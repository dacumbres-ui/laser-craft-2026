## Cuerpo del Post:
If you are looking for a side hustle that combines creativity with a physical product, laser engraving is currently at its most accessible point. 

You no longer need a massive CO2 laser to start. Modern entry-level diode lasers (under 300€) can handle wood, leather, slate, and even coated metals. 

**The Strategy:**
1. Buy a reliable budget machine (Ray5, Creality, etc.).
2. Focus on a niche (Personalized pet tags, wedding favors, custom tools).
3. Sell on Etsy or local Facebook groups.

The ROI is quite fast if you focus on small items that take 5-10 minutes to engrave.
